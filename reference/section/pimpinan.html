base: 4px gutter-sm: 16px gutter-lg: 24px margin-mobile: 16px margin-desktop: 48px max-width: 1440px --- ## Brand & Style The design system is engineered for high-performance SaaS and technology platforms, evoking a sense of precision, reliability, and forward-thinking innovation. The brand personality is "The Sophisticated Architect"—authoritative yet accessible, utilizing a deep professional aesthetic balanced by vibrant, energetic accents. The visual style is **Corporate Modern with Glassmorphism influences**. It leverages deep navy backgrounds to create a sense of infinite space, while UI elements appear as frosted, translucent layers floating above the canvas. This creates a premium, futuristic atmosphere that prioritizes data density and clarity without feeling cluttered. The contrast between the dark base and the lime green accent ensures a dynamic user experience that guides attention to critical actions. ## Colors The palette is anchored in a dark-mode-first philosophy. The **Primary** color is a deep, professional navy used for backgrounds and major structural containers. The **Secondary** (Accent) color is a vibrant lime green, reserved for high-impact calls to action, success states, and key data points. A **Tertiary** sky blue is introduced for secondary actions and information highlights, providing a bridge between the deep navy and the lime green. Neutrals are kept crisp and cool, ranging from offwhite for primary text to muted grays for borders and secondary metadata. Color application should follow a 60-30-10 rule: 60% deep primary, 30% neutral/tertiary accents, and 10% lime green for peak visual emphasis. ## Typography This design system utilizes **Inter** exclusively to maintain a systematic, utilitarian, and clean aesthetic. The typography relies on varying weights and tight letter spacing for headlines to achieve a "tech-editorial" look. Labels and navigation items are frequently set in uppercase with increased letter spacing to create a sense of precision and structure. For mobile devices, large display sizes scale down significantly to ensure legibility and prevent awkward line breaks in narrow viewports. ## Layout & Spacing The layout follows a **Fluid Grid** model with strict 8px increments for internal component spacing. On desktop, the layout is contained within a 12-column grid with a maximum width of 1440px. - **Desktop:** 12 columns, 24px gutters, 48px side margins. - **Tablet:** 8 columns, 16px gutters, 32px side margins. - **Mobile:** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 16px side margins. Content should flow vertically in logical blocks. Use generous white space between sections to prevent "information fatigue" in data-heavy environments. ## Elevation & Depth Visual hierarchy is established through **Glassmorphism and Ambient Glows**. Surfaces do not use traditional drop shadows; instead, they use a combination of: 1. **Backdrop Blurs:** High-level containers (like the navigation bar in the reference) use a `20px` background blur with a semi-transparent navy fill (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.8)`). 2. **Inner Glows:** Active elements utilize a subtle 1px inner border (top-weighted) to simulate light catching the edge of a glass pane. 3. **Tonal Stacking:** Surfaces are defined by color luminosity. The background is the darkest, while cards and modals are 5-10% lighter to indicate "closeness" to the user. ## Shapes The design system uses a **Rounded-XL** language to soften the high-tech aesthetic, making the interface feel approachable and modern. - **Base Radius (Buttons, Inputs):** 0.5rem (8px). - **Large Radius (Cards, Modals):** 1rem (16px). - **Extra Large Radius (Navigation Bars, Containers):** 1.5rem (24px). Pill-shaped elements (Full Round) are reserved strictly for status badges and chips to differentiate them from actionable buttons. ## Components ### Buttons - **Primary:** Solid Lime Green (#274CA5) with dark navy text. No shadow, but a subtle "glow" on hover using a soft external blur of the accent color. - **Secondary:** Outlined with a 1.5px border in Tertiary blue. - **Ghost:** Transparent background with neutral white text, used for less prominent actions. ### Navigation Bar Inspired by the reference image: A floating, high-radius container with a heavy backdrop blur. Active items should feature a "pill" highlight with a high-contrast background (light blue or lime green) to clearly indicate location. ### Input Fields Darker than the surface background to create an "inset" feel. Borders should be subtle gray, turning Lime Green on focus. Use Inter Label-SM for floating labels. ### Cards Cards should utilize the `rounded-lg` (1rem) setting. Backgrounds should be slightly lighter than the main canvas with a very thin (0.5px) border in a semi-transparent neutral to provide definition without looking "boxy." ### Chips & Status Indicators Always pill-shaped. Use Lime Green for "Active/Success," Tertiary Blue for "Info," and a muted coral for "Errors."
